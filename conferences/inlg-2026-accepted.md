# INLG 2026 Accepted Papers

Last updated: 2026-11-02

This tracker collects papers accepted to the 19th International Conference on
Natural Language Generation (INLG 2026). It is compiled from author
announcements and the conference program, and is maintained by the reading
group for session planning.

## Main track

- **A Large-Scale Dataset for Cross-Lingual Multi-Jurisdiction Patent Drafting from Patent Cooperation Treaty Applications** -- Trung Phuong Le, Hirofumi Nonaka, Koji Marusaki, Katsuhito Sudoh, Seiya Kawano
- **Discourse-Aware Summarisation of Long Legal Judgments** -- Ana Belmonte, Ravi Chandran, Mei-Ling Chow
- **Controllable Simplification of Procedural Texts for Low-Resource Languages** -- Jonas Eriksen, Priya Nair
- **Evaluating Faithfulness in Data-to-Text Generation with Contrast Sets** -- Sofia Marchetti, Daniel Okafor
- **A Benchmark for Multilingual Dialogue Summarisation in Contact Centres** -- Hana Kovac, Tomaž Zupan, Elena Petrova
- **Sentence Planning with Retrieval-Augmented Language Models** -- Yusuf Demir, Clara Weiss
- **Learning to Ask Clarifying Questions in Task-Oriented Dialogue** -- Ngozi Adeyemi, Kenji Sato

## System demonstrations

- **An Open Toolkit for Reproducible Human Evaluation of Generated Text** -- Laura Kim, Peter Novak
- **Interactive Report Generation for Structured Data** -- Mateo Alvarez, Ingrid Johansson
